# Interaction Design Foundation

An education organisation for designers, known as IxDF, with a large free library of articles arranged by topic: [ixdf.org/literature/topics](https://ixdf.org/literature/topics).

It calls the library a "Design Compendium". Each topic, such as affordances or Fitts's Law, has an overview page that defines the term and gathers the articles and videos about it. The organisation also sells courses.

For this collection it works as a much larger dictionary. When a term here needs more depth or a second explanation, there is probably a topic page for it. It covers the whole of UX design, so visual vocabulary such as the [[gestalt-principles|Gestalt principles]] and [[visual-hierarchy|visual hierarchy]] is one part among many.
