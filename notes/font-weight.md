# Font weight

The thickness of a typeface's strokes.

Weights are named (light, regular, medium, semibold, bold) and numbered from 100 to 900 in CSS, where 400 is regular and 700 is bold.

Weight is a strong signal for [[typographic-hierarchy|typographic hierarchy]], and it is easy to overspend. Most interfaces need two weights, perhaps three: regular for body, and one or two heavier ones for headings and [[emphasis]]. Adjacent weights such as 500 and 600 are hard to tell apart and should not be asked to carry different meanings.

Not to be confused with [[visual-weight|visual weight]], the general pull of any element. Font weight is one thing that contributes to it.
