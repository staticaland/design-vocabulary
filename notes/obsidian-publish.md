# Obsidian Publish

A paid hosting service from the makers of Obsidian that puts a vault of notes on the web: [obsidian.md/publish](https://obsidian.md/publish).

Obsidian is a note-taking app that works on a folder of plain Markdown files and links them with the `[[link]]` syntax these notes use. Publish is its add-on for sharing those notes. It is priced per site, per month.

It offers a graph view, backlinks, hover previews, and "stacked pages", where a link opens in a pane beside the current note, in the manner of [[andy-matuschak-notes|Andy Matuschak's notes]].

The notes in this collection can be opened as a vault in Obsidian itself, which is free, without using Publish.
