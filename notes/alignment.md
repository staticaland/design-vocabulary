# Alignment

Placing elements so that their edges or centres fall on a shared invisible line.

Elements can align on an edge (left, right, top, bottom), a centre line, or a text [baseline](baseline.md). The eye is very good at detecting near misses: two things that almost line up look like a mistake, while two things that clearly do not line up look like a choice.

Alignment also carries meaning. Things on the same line read as belonging to the same set, so alignment is a quiet form of [grouping](gestalt-principles.md).
