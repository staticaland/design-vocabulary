# Squint test

A quick check of [visual hierarchy](visual-hierarchy.md): squint at the screen, or blur a screenshot, until the detail disappears, and see what still stands out.

Blurring removes the text and leaves only blobs of [visual weight](visual-weight.md) and the spaces between them. That is roughly what a user takes in during the first moment on a page, before reading anything.

Two things should survive:

- **The most important element.** If the heaviest blob is something minor, the hierarchy is wrong.
- **The main groupings.** If the page blurs into one even texture, the [grouping](gestalt-principles.md) is too weak. If it blurs into a dozen separate boxes, there is too much of it.

It can be done by squinting, stepping back from the screen, or applying a blur filter to a screenshot.
