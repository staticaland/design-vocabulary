# Padding

The space between a container's edge and its content, on the inside.

Padding is what keeps text from touching the border of a button or card. It belongs to the container: a background colour fills the padding too.

It is one of three kinds of space in the CSS box model, and the difference matters when diagnosing spacing problems:

- **Padding:** inside the element's border.
- **Margin:** outside the element's border, pushing other elements away.
- **[Gap](gap.md):** between the children of a container, owned by the parent.
