# Nesting

Placing one bounded container inside another: a box in a box.

One level of nesting is ordinary (a [card](card.md) on a page). The trouble starts at two or three: a bordered panel holding a bordered card holding a bordered field. Each level adds its own border and [padding](padding.md), so content is squeezed inwards, edges multiply, and the left edges of the content stop lining up (see [alignment axis](alignment-axis.md)).

Nesting usually appears when hierarchy is expressed only by containment. The alternatives are to group by [proximity](proximity.md), separate with a heading or a divider line, or drop the outer container.
