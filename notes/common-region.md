# Common region

The [[gestalt-principles|Gestalt principle]] that elements sharing a clearly bounded area are perceived as a group.

The boundary can be a border, a background tint, or a [[card]]. Common region is stronger than [[proximity]]: items inside one box read as a group even if items outside it are closer.

That strength has a cost. Every region adds lines and fills, which add [[visual-weight|visual weight]] and clutter. So a region is kept subtle, and used only where spacing alone does not make the grouping clear.

Regions nested inside regions lose their meaning quickly. See [[nesting]].
