# Visual polish

The last layer of refinement in a UI: not what it does or how it is laid out, but whether the details are consistent and deliberate.

Polish is mostly the absence of small errors: a label two pixels off its neighbour, three slightly different greys, a heading that is bold in one place and semibold in another. Each one is minor. Together they make an interface feel careless, and users read carelessness as untrustworthiness.

Polish is checked on the rendered result, not the code. Code can look tidy and still render badly.
