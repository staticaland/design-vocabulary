# Accent colour

The one distinctive colour in a [palette](colour-palette.md), set against the neutrals and reserved for the things that most need attention, usually interactive elements and the primary action.

An accent works by scarcity. Against a mostly neutral interface, a single saturated colour is a strong form of [emphasis](emphasis.md), and it can teach a rule: "this colour means you can act on it". Both effects weaken each time the accent is used for something else, such as decoration, headings, or a second kind of meaning.

A common rule of thumb for proportions is 60–30–10: a dominant neutral, a secondary tone, and an accent on about a tenth of the surface.
