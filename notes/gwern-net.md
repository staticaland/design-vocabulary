# Gwern.net

The personal website of the pseudonymous writer Gwern, known for long essays that are revised over many years and for its link previews: [gwern.net](https://gwern.net/).

The site's stated ideal is "long content": writing meant to last and improve over decades, instead of being posted once and left. Its subjects range over statistics, psychology, technology, and more. Design is not one of them.

It is here for its interface. Hovering over a link opens a popup with the linked page or an excerpt from it, so a reader can check a reference without leaving the page. The site documents this and its other design decisions at length on a page of its own.

The hover previews on this collection's site are the same idea in a much simpler form.
