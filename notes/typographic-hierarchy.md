# Typographic hierarchy

[[visual-hierarchy|Visual hierarchy]] applied to text: using size, [[font-weight|weight]], and colour so that a reader can tell headings, body text, and labels apart at a glance.

The usual roles:

- **Headings:** name a page or section. Larger or heavier.
- **Body:** the text meant to be read. The baseline everything else is measured against.
- **Labels:** short text that names something else, such as a form field, a column, or a button. Often smaller or quieter.

A clear hierarchy uses a limited set of styles, each tied to a role. The warning sign is styles that are nearly the same, such as 15px and 16px, or medium and semibold, used for the same job. A difference too small to notice does not communicate, and reads as an error.
