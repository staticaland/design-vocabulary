# Sources

An entry point, not a concept. It lists the outside sources behind this collection: where its vocabulary comes from, and what its format is modelled on.

## Skills for coding agents

The collection began as a way to understand these two. Each has a map from its terms to the notes.

- [[polish-ui]]: a checklist for reviewing a UI's visual polish
- [[impeccable|Impeccable]]: a larger design skill, and the source of the [[steering-words|steering words]]

## On design

Where to read more about the concepts.

- [[refactoring-ui|Refactoring UI]]: a book of practical tactics, covering much of the same ground as these notes
- [[laws-of-ux|Laws of UX]]: one page per principle of perception and behaviour
- [[practical-typography|Practical Typography]]: a web book on type
- [[cant-unsee|Can't Unsee]]: a game for training the eye
- [[nielsen-norman-group|Nielsen Norman Group]]: research-based articles on usability
- [[interaction-design-foundation|Interaction Design Foundation]]: a large library arranged by topic

## On linked notes

What the format of this collection, and of its website, is modelled on.

- [[andy-matuschak-notes|Andy Matuschak's notes]]: one idea per note, with structure from links
- [[maggie-appleton-garden|Maggie Appleton's digital garden]]: notes that grow over time
- [[gwern-net|Gwern.net]]: previews when hovering over a link
- [[quartz|Quartz]]: a free tool for publishing notes like these
- [[obsidian-publish|Obsidian Publish]]: the hosted equivalent
