# Cap height

The height of a font's capital letters, measured from the [[baseline]] to the top of a flat capital such as `H`.

In most fonts the capitals are a little shorter than the [[ascender|ascenders]] of letters such as `d` and `k`.

Cap height matters most when something has to be centred against text. The box a browser draws around a line of text is taller than the letters, with room above and below, so centring an icon on that box rarely looks right. Centring it on the cap height does, when the text is in capitals or starts with one. Beside lowercase text, the [[x-height]] is often the better target. Both are forms of [[optical-alignment|optical alignment]].

It is also the height to match when a logo or a badge sits in a line of capitals.
