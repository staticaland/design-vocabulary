# Visual weight

How strongly an element pulls the eye, compared with what is around it.

Weight is not one property but the sum of several. An element is heavier when it is larger, darker or more saturated, filled instead of outlined, thickly bordered, higher in [[contrast]] with its background, or surrounded by more empty space.

Two rules follow:

- **Weight should track importance.** The heaviest thing on the screen should be the most important. A common fault is a heavy secondary element, such as a solid red "delete" button or a thick-bordered sidebar, outshouting the primary content.
- **A part should not outweigh its whole.** A badge should not be heavier than the card it sits in, nor a table's borders heavier than its data.
