# Emphasis

Making one element stand out from its neighbours, by bold, colour, size, or another increase in [[visual-weight|visual weight]].

Emphasis is relative. It works only against a background of things that are not emphasised, which gives the rule: if everything is emphasised, nothing is. A page with five bold phrases, three colours, and two large buttons has no emphasis, only noise.

Two disciplines keep it working:

- **Sparingly:** one primary action per view, and few emphasised phrases per block of text.
- **Consistently:** a given kind of emphasis means the same thing each time. If bold marks key terms, it does not also mark warnings.

The reverse technique is often better: instead of making the important thing louder, make the unimportant things quieter (de-emphasis).
