# Proximity

The [[gestalt-principles|Gestalt principle]] that elements placed close together are perceived as a group.

It is the cheapest grouping tool there is, since it needs no extra ink: no lines, no boxes, only space. The rule that follows is that related items sit closer to each other than to unrelated ones. A form label must be nearer to its own field than to the field above. A heading must be nearer to the text it introduces than to the text before it.

The classic failure is uniform spacing, where everything is the same distance from everything else and the structure has to be worked out by reading.

Proximity is tried first. Only when it is not enough is a stronger tool such as [[common-region|common region]] added.
