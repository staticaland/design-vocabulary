# Nielsen Norman Group

A user-experience research and consulting firm, often written NN/g, that publishes a large library of free articles and videos: [nngroup.com](https://www.nngroup.com/).

It was founded by Jakob Nielsen and Don Norman, two of the people who established usability as a field. Besides the articles it sells reports, training courses, and a certification.

Its articles are a standard reference, and are based on research with users. The best known is "10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design". Many terms are explained there at more length, and with more evidence, than in a short note.

Its subject is usability: whether people can understand and use an interface. That sits one level above these notes. A screen can be well polished and still hard to use, or the reverse. NN/g does cover visual design where it affects use, including the [[gestalt-principles|Gestalt principles]] and [[visual-hierarchy|visual hierarchy]].
